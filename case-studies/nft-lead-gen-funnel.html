Case Study

Global Lead-Gen Funnel for an NFT Gaming Launch

ClickFunnels · Meta & TikTok Ads · EmailOctopus · Ninja Software, 2022

Brief

Built while working as Digital Marketing Specialist at Ninja Software: a full lead-generation funnel for an NFT gaming project's community launch, taking cold social traffic through to an engaged Discord/Telegram community and an owned email list. The project name stays out of this write-up, but the funnel mechanics are exactly what was built and run.

1. Finding the audience before spending on ads

Rather than guessing where to advertise, the first step was research: mapping out where NFT gaming audiences were actually active across social platforms, and ranking the top markets by engagement — the Philippines and several other Southeast Asian and emerging markets came out near the top. That ranking is what decided where ad spend and creative localisation went, instead of defaulting to the largest or most obvious markets.

2. A landing page per audience, not one page for everyone

Each priority market got its own ClickFunnels landing page, built to match how that audience actually talks about and engages with NFT gaming rather than a single generic pitch translated across the board. The offer on every page was the same lead magnet: a free downloadable ebook — a game guide and story primer — in exchange for an email address.

3. Paid social to drive cold traffic

Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ad campaigns pointed cold audiences at these localised landing pages, with creative and messaging adjusted per platform and per market rather than run as one identical campaign everywhere.

4. What happens the moment someone opts in

From there, EmailOctopus automation took over: an instant welcome/thank-you email, the ebook download link, and an invitation into the project's Discord and Telegram communities — turning a one-off lead into someone who'd actually joined the community, not just a name on a list.

5. Keeping the list warm after the first email

Beyond the initial welcome sequence, the list received ongoing promotional emails and Gleam-run giveaways — the kind of recurring, low-friction incentive that keeps an audience engaged with a project between major announcements, rather than going quiet after the first touch.

What this demonstrates

  • 01Research-led targeting — deciding where to spend ad budget from actual audience data, not assumption.
  • 02Funnel-building across tools — ClickFunnels for landing pages, EmailOctopus for automation, Gleam for incentives, tied together into one flow.
  • 03Multi-platform paid social — running and localising creative across Meta and TikTok rather than a single one-size-fits-all campaign.
  • 04Community-first conversion — treating Discord/Telegram membership, not just an email address, as the real measure of a converted lead.
